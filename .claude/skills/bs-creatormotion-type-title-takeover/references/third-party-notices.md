# Runtime attribution

Inter and Noto Sans SC provide the main typography; IBM Plex Mono provides numeric labels. These Google Fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Smiley Sans (Deyi Hei) v2.0.1 by atelierAnchor is the default typeface for Chinese content; it is bundled unmodified as smiley-sans-oblique.woff2 under the SIL Open Font License. Their local files and licenses are included in runtime-assets/fonts/ to make rendering deterministic. Legacy Archivo files remain only for compatibility; they are not selected by the active typography. The font licenses are inter-OFL.txt, noto-sans-sc-OFL.txt, ibmplexmono-OFL.txt, smiley-sans-OFL.txt and archivo-OFL.txt in that directory. GSAP is provided under its standard license; its copyright and license header remain in the distributed library. HyperFrames, Node.js and FFmpeg are external tools required by the workflow.

Reference creator videos, their faces, words, logos and interface skins are not included. Generated raster defaults listed in default-images/manifest.json are included with their generation provenance and checksums. Reference footage, presenter takes and preview media remain excluded. User replacements take precedence over bundled defaults.
